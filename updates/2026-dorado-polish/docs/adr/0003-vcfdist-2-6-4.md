# Score every Arm with vcfdist v2.6.4, not the paper's v2.3.3

We score every Arm with vcfdist v2.6.4 (all vcfdist releases are tagged pre-release; this is the newest). It includes scoring changes since the paper's v2.3.3, notably excluding variants on BED region borders (v2.4.0) and new sync-group and precision-recall rules (v2.5.0), as well as a haploid segfault fix (v2.6.2). We chose the latest version over reproducing the paper's exact numbers. As a result, Arm A will not match the eLife figures exactly. The Read sets also differ: they are depth-capped with `rasusa aln` rather than randomly subsampled (ADR-0005).

## Consequences

- Never pass `-s`: in v2.6+ it means `--max-supercluster-size`, not `--smallest-variant`.
- `query.tsv` column `ERR_TYPE` is `ERRTYPE` in v2.6.4. The `precision-recall-summary.tsv` columns are unchanged.
